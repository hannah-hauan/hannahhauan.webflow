Learning Design

After five years in the classroom, I transitioned into Learning Design to make digital learning just as engaging. I specialize in STEM education, crafting interactive eLearning that turns complex topics into hands-on experiences. From accessibility challenges for Fortune 500 teams to phonics lessons and real-world chemistry, I design learning that’s immersive, effective, and fun.
